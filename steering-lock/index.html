Two-minute setup

Lock it. Unlock it. Both in seconds.

To lock

Step 1

Loop the padded cable around the steering wheel rim.

Step 2

Insert the lock head into the seatbelt buckle socket until it clicks.

To unlock

Step 1

Insert the key into the lock head, turn, and push down.

Step 2

Pull the cable outward while pushing. It releases cleanly.
