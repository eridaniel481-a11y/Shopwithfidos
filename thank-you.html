Common questions
I haven't been contacted yet, is my order still valid?

Yes. If it's been more than 24 hours and you haven't heard from us, message us on WhatsApp with your reference number and we'll check immediately.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, just let us know during the confirmation call or by WhatsApp before dispatch. Once it's on the way, changes get harder to make.

What if I'm not around when the agent arrives?

Let us know in advance and we'll reschedule. Missed deliveries with no notice can delay your order and add extra delivery costs.
