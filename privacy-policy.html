Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ShopWithFidos ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you place an order on this website, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

When you submit an order through our website, we collect the details you provide directly:

  • First and last name
  • WhatsApp number
  • Email address, if you choose to provide one
  • Delivery address, state, and city or LGA
  • The package or product you selected

We do not collect payment card details on this website, since ShopWithFidos operates on a Pay on Delivery basis. Payment is made directly to the delivery agent, not through this site.

How we use your information

  • To process and confirm your order
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email regarding delivery
  • To arrange delivery with our courier or dispatch partners
  • To send you an order confirmation, if you provided an email address
  • To improve our service and respond to questions or complaints

Who we share it with

We share order details only with the people directly involved in fulfilling your order, such as our delivery agents, and with the service providers that power this website, including our hosting provider (Netlify) and our email delivery provider (Resend). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

If we run advertising on Meta platforms (Facebook/Instagram), we may share limited, de-identified information, such as a hashed version of your WhatsApp number, with Meta to help measure the effectiveness of our ads. This never includes your name, address, or any readable contact detail.

How long we keep your information

We retain order information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil your order, handle any after-sales questions, and meet our own bookkeeping needs, typically no longer than is necessary for these purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal or bookkeeping requirement to retain order records. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us, including transmitting it over secure (HTTPS) connections and limiting who has access to order data. No method of transmission over the internet is ever 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to the best of our ability.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
